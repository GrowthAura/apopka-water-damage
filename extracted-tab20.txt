[HEADING_3] Industry Standards
[NORMAL_TEXT] Water damage work follows the IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration, and mold work follows the IICRC S520 standard. Those standards set out how to classify water, when to remove materials, how to set up drying and how to know when a structure is truly dry.
[HEADING_3] Measure, Don't Guess
[NORMAL_TEXT] Moisture meters, thermal imaging and daily readings guide every decision. Equipment comes out when the readings say the home is dry, not when the calendar says so.
[HEADING_3] Save What Can Be Saved
