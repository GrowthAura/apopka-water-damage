[HEADING_2] Our Ceiling Water Damage Repair Process
[HEADING_3] 1. Make It Safe
[NORMAL_TEXT] Power to affected fixtures is shut off, people and belongings are moved, and any sagging section holding water is drained in a controlled way.
[HEADING_3] 2. Find and Stop the Source

[HEADING_3] 3. Moisture Mapping
[NORMAL_TEXT] Thermal imaging and moisture meters show how far water spread across the ceiling, into the walls below and through the insulation. Water often travels along joists or trusses before it shows through.

[NORMAL_TEXT] New drywall is hung and finished, texture is matched (knockdown, popcorn or smooth), and the ceiling is primed with a stain-blocking primer and painted. New insulation is installed in the attic.
