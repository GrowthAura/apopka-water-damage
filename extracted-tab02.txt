[HEADING_2] Wet Vac vs Truck Mount Extraction: Why It Matters
[NORMAL_TEXT] Many homeowners start with a shop vac, and that is a reasonable first move for a small spill. For anything bigger, the difference in equipment is huge.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Shop vac or wet vac: Holds a few gallons, needs constant emptying and pulls water only from the surface. It cannot reach water held in carpet pad or under flooring.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Portable professional extractor: Much stronger suction and larger capacity, used for upstairs rooms, condos and tight spaces.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Truck-mounted extraction: The strongest option. Water goes straight into the truck's tank, the unit runs continuously and the suction pulls water from deep inside carpet and pad.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Submersible pumps: Used when water is several inches deep, such as a flooded garage or sunken living room.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Using the right water extraction service from the start shortens drying time by days and is often the difference between saving flooring and replacing it.
