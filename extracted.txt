[HEADING_2] Water Damage Categories: Clean, Gray and Black Water
[NORMAL_TEXT] The IICRC S500 standard sorts water losses into three categories, and the category decides how your home is cleaned.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Category 1, clean water: Comes from a sanitary source such as a burst supply pipe, water heater or ice-maker line. Usually the fastest to dry if handled quickly.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Category 2, gray water: Contains some contamination, such as washing machine discharge, dishwasher overflow or AC condensate that has sat in a pan. Needs cleaning and antimicrobial treatment.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Category 3, black water: Sewage, septic backups and outdoor flood water. Porous materials it touches usually have to be removed, and the area must be sanitized before drying.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Clean water does not stay clean. After about 48 hours in a warm Apopka home, Category 1 water can degrade into Category 2 or 3, which is one more reason to call right away.
