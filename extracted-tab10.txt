[HEADING_2] Flood Water Category 3: Why Flood Cleanup Is Different
[NORMAL_TEXT] Not all water damage is the same. Flood water from outside is always treated as Category 3, or black water, under the IICRC S500 standard. It can carry sewage from overflowing lines and septic systems, fertilizer and pesticides from lawns, oil and chemicals from streets, animal waste and bacteria.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Because of that, flood cleanup follows stricter rules than a clean-water leak:
[NORMAL_TEXT] Porous materials it touched, such as carpet, pad, drywall, insulation and particleboard, are usually removed and discarded.
[NORMAL_TEXT] All remaining surfaces are cleaned and disinfected before drying begins.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Crews wear protective equipment, and the work area is controlled.
[NORMAL_TEXT] Belongings that soaked up flood water are sorted into what can be cleaned and what must be thrown out.
[NORMAL_TEXT] This is why rising water cleanup should never be treated like a burst pipe or appliance leak.
