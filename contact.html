What info should you have ready when you call?

Tell us where the water is coming from, which rooms are wet, whether it is clean or sewage, and whether the water is shut off. That helps the crew bring the right equipment.

Helpful details include:

  • Your address and the best phone number to reach you.
  • The source of the water, if you know it: pipe, AC, water heater, appliance, toilet, roof, storm or sewage.
  • How many rooms are affected and what flooring is in them.
  • Whether the water is still flowing or has been shut off.
  • Roughly when the leak started or when you found it.
  • Whether anyone in the home is elderly, very young or has health concerns.
  • Whether you plan to file an insurance claim.

While You Wait for the Crew

  • Shut off the main water valve or the fixture valve.
  • Turn off power to wet rooms at the breaker panel.
  • Move valuables, electronics and rugs to a dry area.
  • Take photos and video for your insurance claim.
  • Stay out of any water that might be sewage or flood water.
