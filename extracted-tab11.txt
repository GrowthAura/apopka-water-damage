[HEADING_2] Septic Backup Cleanup Apopka FL: Why Septic Systems Back Up
[HEADING_3] Full Tank
[NORMAL_TEXT] Septic tanks need pumping every few years. When solids build up, they block the outlet and waste backs up into the house.
[HEADING_3] Saturated Drain Field
[NORMAL_TEXT] Apopka's high water table and heavy summer rain can saturate the drain field, so effluent has nowhere to go. Backups after big storms are very common in septic homes.
[HEADING_3] Clogged or Collapsed Lines
[NORMAL_TEXT] Roots, grease and non-flushable items block the pipe between the house and the tank. Older clay or cast-iron lines can crack or collapse.
[HEADING_3] Failed Pump or Alarm
[NORMAL_TEXT] Some systems use a lift pump. If the pump fails, the tank fills and backs up into the home.
[HEADING_3] Overuse
[NORMAL_TEXT] Running several loads of laundry, long showers and a dishwasher in a short time can overload a system that is already near capacity.

[NORMAL_TEXT] Summer storms are the most common trigger for septic backups in Apopka.
