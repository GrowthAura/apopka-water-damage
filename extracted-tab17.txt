[HEADING_2] Flat Roof Leaks on Commercial Buildings
[NORMAL_TEXT] Many commercial buildings in Apopka have flat or low-slope roofs. Water ponds on these roofs after heavy rain and finds its way in at seams, roof drains, HVAC curbs and parapet walls. Drying the ceiling cavity, insulation and anything below is important even after the roof is repaired.
